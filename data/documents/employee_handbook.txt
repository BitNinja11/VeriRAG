WELCOME TO NORTHWIND SYSTEMS

This handbook summarises the policies that apply to you. Where this handbook
differs from an official policy document, the official policy governs.

TIME OFF

Full-time staff get 24 days of paid annual leave each year. You accrue these
days over the year at a rate of 2 days per month worked.

You can carry up to 10 unused days into the following year. Anything above
that is lost at the end of December.

WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one
hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

REMOTE WORK

Employees may work remotely up to 8 days per month with manager approval.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred.
Reimbursement is processed in the following payroll cycle.
