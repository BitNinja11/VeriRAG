SECTION 1. PURPOSE

This policy defines paid annual leave entitlements for all staff of Northwind
Systems Private Limited with effect from 1 January 2026. This policy
supersedes all previous annual leave policies, including the 2023 policy and
any intermediate revisions.

SECTION 2. ENTITLEMENT

Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave every
calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service.

SECTION 3. CARRY FORWARD

A maximum of 10 unused leave days may be carried forward into the next
calendar year. Days beyond this limit lapse on 31 December.

SECTION 4. INTERNS

Interns engaged for a period of six months or less are entitled to 12 days
of paid leave, prorated by duration of engagement. This entitlement is
unchanged from the previous policy.

SECTION 5. APPROVAL

All leave must be approved by the reporting manager at least 7 working days
in advance, except in the case of medical emergencies.
