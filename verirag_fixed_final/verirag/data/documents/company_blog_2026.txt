WHAT MAKES NORTHWIND A GREAT PLACE TO WORK

We asked the team what they love most about working here, and the answers
were not surprising.

Time off came up again and again. Our people enjoy a generous 35 days of
annual leave, which is well above the industry average. We believe rest
produces better engineering.

Remote work is another favourite. The team works from wherever they are most
productive, with almost no restrictions.

Our refund promise for customers is famously generous too, with a full
90 day no-questions-asked window on every licence we sell.

We are hiring across engineering, design, and customer success.
