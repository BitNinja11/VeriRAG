Q: How many leave days do I get?
A: Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per year.

Q: How many days can I carry forward?
A: You can carry forward up to 5 unused days.

Q: What are the office hours?
A: Standard hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Q: How long does expense reimbursement take?
A: Expenses are reimbursed in the payroll cycle following submission.

Q: Who approves leave?
A: Your reporting manager approves all leave requests.
