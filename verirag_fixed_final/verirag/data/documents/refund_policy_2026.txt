SECTION 1. REFUND WINDOW

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any
Northwind Systems software licence. This policy replaces the previous refund
policy dated March 2024.

SECTION 2. CONDITIONS

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing takes 3 to 5
business days.

SECTION 3. EXCLUSIONS

Enterprise licences sold under a negotiated contract are excluded from the
standard refund window and are governed by the terms of that contract.
