CAFETERIA

The office cafeteria operates from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays. Subsidised
lunch is served between 12:30 PM and 2:30 PM.

PARKING

Parking passes are issued on a first come first served basis. The waiting
list is currently approximately three weeks.

VISITOR ACCESS

All visitors must be registered at reception at least one day in advance.
Visitor badges must be returned on exit.

MAINTENANCE

The building undergoes scheduled maintenance on the first Saturday of every
month. Office access is restricted on those days.
